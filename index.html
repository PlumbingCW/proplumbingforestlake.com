Plumbing Emergencies in Forest Lake, Handled Any Hour

An emergency plumber earns the name when the problem cannot wait for business hours. Water spreading across a floor, sewage in a basement drain, a water heater leaking onto the slab, or a sump pump that quit in a storm all fit. Our 24/7 line is open every day of the year, and the first thing the dispatcher asks is what you can safely do while you wait.

Water you cannot stop

A burst pipe or failed valve calls for the main shutoff first. See emergency burst pipe repair.

Sewage or a backed-up drain

Standing water in a floor drain means the main line is blocked. Learn what to do on the emergency drain cleaning page.

No hot water or a leaking tank

A failed tank in winter is urgent. See water heater repair and replacement.

A basement taking on water

A dead sump pump during a storm needs fast help. See sump pump repair.

Gas is different. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before anyone else. A plumber can help once the building is safe.

Local knowledge

Forest Lake Plumbing Has Its Own Quirks

Forest Lake sits on the shore of the largest lake in Washington County, about 2,220 acres, with the Comfort Lake-Forest Lake Watershed District managing water that drains toward it. Lakeside homes and low lots tend to see wet basements and sump pump work more than higher ground does.

City water here comes from the Mt. Simon and Quaternary Buried Artesian aquifers and is softened at the treatment plants to an average of 5.5 to 6.5 grains per gallon, so scale is lighter than in many places. The city also reports 408 lead service lines in its 2023 inventory, so older water service lines are worth a look. See water service line repair.

The city maintains 93 miles of public sanitary sewer and 46 lift stations. When a backup happens, the first question is whether the blockage is in the public main or the private pipe from the main to the house. The city asks residents who cannot tell to call its Utilities Department before hiring a drain company, which is covered on the sewer line repair page.

City code also bars sump pump and footing drain water from the sanitary sewer, so any pump work has to route water outside. We follow that rule on every sump job, and the basement waterproofing page explains the larger picture.
