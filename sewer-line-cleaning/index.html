Emergency Sewer Line Cleaning in Forest Lake, MN

When the main sewer line is blocked, every drain in the house is affected, and sewage can rise in the lowest fixture. Our 24/7 line is open for this. Stop using water, keep the toilets unflushed, and wait for the plumber before pouring anything down a drain.

Sewer line cleaning is the clearing of the main drain that carries all wastewater from your house toward the city sewer or septic tank. It uses larger machines than a sink clog and often starts at a cleanout outside or in the basement.

Sewer backup: how to tell it is the main line

A fixture clog affects one sink or toilet. A main line clog affects the whole system. The tub gurgles when you flush, water backs up into the shower, or a basement floor drain fills when the laundry runs.

If the lowest drain in the house is the first one to show water, the blockage is almost certainly in the main line or the city main, not in a fixture.

Where the cleaning starts

The plumber looks for the main cleanout, a capped pipe in the basement, crawlspace, or yard. A machine goes in through the cleanout, which avoids pulling a toilet. If there is no cleanout, access can come through a toilet or a vent stack, and the plumber may recommend installing a cleanout so future cleaning is easier.

Large cable machines carry heavier cables and cutter heads that can grind through roots and paper. When the buildup is grease or scale, hydro jetting cleans the wall of the pipe more completely, and the camera inspection shows what is actually in there.

Forest Lake and the roots problem

The City of Forest Lake's Sewer Department points to tree roots as a common cause of blockages in older service pipes. Lakeside neighborhoods and older streets near downtown have large trees, and roots find small gaps in joints. A cutter clears them for now, but they return unless the joint is repaired.

If a line has been cut several times in a few years, ask the plumber to run a camera and note where the roots enter. A short repair at the joint is often cheaper than a lifetime of cleanings, and sewer line repair explains the options.

Call the city first when the cause is unclear

The city asks residents who cannot tell whether a backup is in their service pipe or the public main to call its Utilities Department before hiring a drain company. A Public Works employee can check the main, and that may spare you a service call. If the main is clear, the blockage is yours to clear.

After a service pipe is cleaned, the city asks owners to let them know, because clearing the lateral can push solids into the main. Mentioning it also helps the city decide whether to check the main again.

What the city asks of residents

According to the City of Forest Lake, wipes, feminine hygiene products, paper towels, floss, and cat litter do not belong in the sewer. When a backup is reported, the city checks the public main first, and the pipe from the main to your house is the owner's to maintain.
