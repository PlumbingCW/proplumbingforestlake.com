How the seasons shape plumbing in Columbus

Spring thaw puts groundwater near the surface and tests sump pumps and septic fields. Summer brings storms and heavy irrigation. Fall is the time to drain outdoor lines and check the water heater. Winter freezes unheated pipes, well houses, and exposed lines. Each season brings a different call.

Wells and septic: what the city says

The City of Columbus provides municipal water and sewer only in its Freeway District along the I-35 corridor, and the city reported 68 connections in 2024. The rest of Columbus, including rural residential and low-density areas, uses private wells and subsurface sewage treatment systems.

The city advises testing private wells every year for coliform bacteria and nitrates, and says free test kits are available at City Hall, with testing at the Anoka County Government Center. Unused wells should be sealed by a licensed well contractor, and Anoka County offers a cost-share program and low-interest loans for failing wells and septic systems.

Anoka County, not Washington County

Columbus is in Anoka County. Washington County septic rules apply to Forest Lake and Hugo, but not here, so ask Columbus City Hall about septic requirements for your property. The city uses a contracted building official for permits, and homeowners doing their own work complete a Homeowner Contractor Form. For plumbing specifically, call City Hall or check the state Department of Labor and Industry.

What the area looks like

Columbus was a township until it incorporated as a city in 2006, partly over concerns that Forest Lake might annex parts of it. The Carlos Avery Wildlife Area covers a large part of the city, Rice Creek flows through it, and Running Aces Harness Park sits near the freeway. Linwood Township lies to the north.

Because the city and Forest Lake share ZIP 55025, many Columbus addresses carry a Forest Lake mailing address. The ZIP 55014 is also used for parts of the city. Whichever your mailing city, the 24/7 line covers you, and the water softener and filtration page explains test-first treatment for wells.

Nearby towns we also serve

Wyoming adjoins Columbus to the north, and Hugo is close by to the southeast. Both have their own pages with local details, and the Wyoming page covers Chisago County rules.

Checking a plumber's license

According to the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry, plumbing contractors must hold a state license, with a responsible master plumber and a surety bond. The license lookup at dli.mn.gov lets you confirm any plumber before you hire.
