Wyoming is in Chisago County

Wyoming sits in Chisago County, not Washington County like Forest Lake. That matters for permits and septic rules, because the county sets them. If your home is on a septic system, ask the City of Wyoming or Chisago County which rules apply. The Washington County septic program discussed on some of our pages does not cover Wyoming addresses.

The city's water and sewer arrangements vary by address. Some properties are on municipal service, and others may use wells and septic. Ask the City of Wyoming which applies to yours, because it decides whether a backup is a city main question or a private line question.

Housing and what it means for plumbing

The 2020 Census counted 2,995 housing units in Wyoming, with a vacancy rate under 3 percent, and the ZIP 55092 data from zip-codes.com shows around 89 percent of occupied homes are owner-occupied. Owner-occupied homes tend to have owner-managed water heaters and pipes, and the decision to repair or replace is usually the owner's alone.

Wyoming was platted in 1869 when the St. Paul and Duluth Railroad reached the site, and the South Branch of the Sunrise River runs through it. Older homes near the original townsite are more likely to have older pipes than the newer subdivisions. A plumber can identify the material when they arrive, and repiping is worth knowing about if you buy older.
