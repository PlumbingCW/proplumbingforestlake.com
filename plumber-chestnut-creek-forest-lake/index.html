Where Chestnut Creek is

Local development reporting lists Chestnut Creek among Forest Lake's housing developments, off Harrow Avenue, with single-family homes of three to four bedrooms. New-home listings place it on the south side of the city. The same report said the developer was expected to seek final platting of another 112 lots, which suggests the subdivision was built in phases.

One caution applies to the address. ZIP code 55025 is shared by Forest Lake and Columbus, so a 55025 mailing address does not by itself show which city a home is in. The Columbus page explains the overlap. Check your property tax statement or the City if you are unsure.

Verified, general and unknown

Only a few facts about Chestnut Creek are documented, so this page keeps them separate from general advice.

  • Verified: the name, the Harrow Avenue location, single-family homes of three to four bedrooms, and phased development.
  • General: a home from the last several years usually still needs its water heater tracked, its shutoffs located and its drains kept clear.
  • Unknown: the build year of any one home, its pipe materials, and the builder. Sources disagree on the builder, so none is named here.

Water and sewer basics for a Forest Lake address

According to the City of Forest Lake Water Department, the city draws from the Mt. Simon and Quaternary Buried Artesian aquifers and softens the water at its treatment plants. Whether a particular home connects to city water and sewer is shown on the utility bill, and this page does not assume it.

The same city sewer page says that when a backup is reported the city checks the public main first, and the owner maintains the pipe from the main to the house. If your lateral is the issue, the sewer line repair page explains the process.

Practical steps for a newer single-family home

  • Photograph the water heater label so the age is on record.
  • Locate the main shutoff and each fixture valve before you need them.
  • If a builder warranty may still apply, read its terms before you pay for a repair.
  • Keep wipes and paper towels out of the toilet, since the city lists them as items that do not belong in the sewer.
