Sewer backup: what to do first

A sewer backup is different from a clogged fixture. The signs are wastewater rising in the lowest drain in the house, several fixtures draining badly together, or a smell of sewage coming from floor drains.

Do this now

Stop all water use. Every flush or load of laundry adds to the backup.

Keep children and pets away. Sewage carries bacteria.

  • If the water is only in a basement floor drain, do not walk through it with the power on in that area.
  • Call the plumber and ask about the camera visit.

Which part of the line is yours?

In Forest Lake the City Sewer Department maintains the public sanitary sewer main. Its guidance is that when a backup is reported, staff check the main through a manhole, and if the main is clear the blockage sits in the pipe between the main and the house. That pipe belongs to the property owner.

That shapes the first call. If you cannot tell where the problem sits, the city asks residents to call its Utilities Department before hiring a drain company, and the city notes it has bought No Fault Sewer Backup Coverage that may reimburse damage from a city-maintained main backup. Details are on the city Sewer Department page.

How sewer damage is found

A sewer camera on a push cable runs through the line and shows the inside of the pipe on a monitor. It shows roots, cracks, offset joints, bellies where water pools, and collapses. The plumber marks the location above ground and measures the depth, so any repair is planned, not guessed.

If the camera shows only buildup, sewer line cleaning may be enough. If it shows damage, the options below apply. A planned inspection before buying a house is covered separately on the sewer inspection page.

Roots, old pipe, and what to expect in Forest Lake

The city sewer page names tree roots as a common cause of blockages in older service lines. Roots enter at joints and cracks, grow into a mass, and catch paper. A line cleared of roots without repairing the entry point will tend to fill again.

Older clay, cast iron, and Orangeburg pipe fail in different ways. Clay tends to crack at joints, cast iron scales and can rot out, and Orangeburg deforms. The age of your home and the camera view tell the plumber which of these you have.

What decides the price of sewer line repair

Repairs run from small to large, and the camera finding drives the choice.

Length of damageA short spot repair costs less than a long run.

What the city asks of residents

According to the City of Forest Lake, wipes, feminine hygiene products, paper towels, floss, and cat litter do not belong in the sewer. When a backup is reported, the city checks the public main first, and the pipe from the main to your house is the owner's to maintain.
