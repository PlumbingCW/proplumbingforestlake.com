Most Hugo homes are newer, and that changes the plumbing

Census counts show 2,174 housing units in Hugo in 2000, 5,189 in 2010, and 6,148 in 2020. By that math, nearly two thirds of the homes standing in 2020 were added after 2000. The city went from a village of French Canadian settlers to a suburb, and the housing followed.

Newer homes mean different problems than a century-old town would have. Supply lines are more likely to be PEX or copper, so repiping is rarely the issue. The calls tend toward water heaters at the end of their first life, builder-grade fixtures that wear out, and sump systems in new construction.

Sump pumps and wet basements near Oneka Lake

Hugo has several lakes, with Oneka Lake the largest within the city and Rice Lake just to the south. Homes near water or on low ground can see groundwater rise after heavy rain or spring thaw. A working sump pump is the front line, and a backup power source is worth considering.

Sump discharge rules depend on where your home connects. Forest Lake's code bars sump water from its sanitary sewer, and Hugo has its own rules. Ask the City of Hugo what applies to your address, and read the sump pump page for the general approach.

City sewer, wells, and septic in Hugo

Parts of Hugo have municipal service and others do not. We have not verified the exact service boundaries, so ask the City of Hugo whether your address is on public water and sewer. Properties on septic fall under the Washington County septic program, which requires a compliance inspection when a property is sold unless one was filed in the last three years.

For a home on a well, a water test is the right first step before any softener or filter. See the water softener and filtration page for how to decide.

Getting around Hugo

Highway 61 runs north and south through the city, and County Road 8 links it to Interstate 35E just west. The city lies between about 120th Street North and 180th Street North, with Elmcrest Avenue and Keystone Avenue as rough east and west edges. Lino Lakes is nearby to the west and south. We do not promise a specific arrival time, but same-day appointments are offered when available.
