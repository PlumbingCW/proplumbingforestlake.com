How to reach us

The best way to reach a plumber is by phone. The line is open 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays. You can also email the address above for non-urgent questions, but a plumbing problem is faster by phone.

What to tell the dispatcher

  • Your address and the nearest cross street or ZIP code.
  • What is happening, such as a leak, a backup, or no hot water.
  • Where the water is coming from and whether you have shut it off.
  • Whether you are on city water and sewer or a private well and septic system.
  • How old the fixture or water heater is, if you know.

The dispatcher will give you safety steps based on standard practice and a clear price before work starts. Same-day appointments are offered when available.

If you are not sure the problem needs a plumber yet, read about burst pipes, clogged drains, or water heater trouble first. The service areas page lists the towns we cover.
