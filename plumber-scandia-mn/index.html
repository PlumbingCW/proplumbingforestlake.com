Scandia: a Swedish settlement on the St. Croix

Scandia is believed to be Minnesota's first Swedish immigrant settlement. The first log cabin went up on the shore of Hay Lake in 1850, and the Elim Lutheran Church sanctuary followed in 1856. It became a city on January 1, 2007, after years as New Scandia Township.

Landmarks include the Gammelgården Museum, the Erickson Log House built in 1868, and Hay Lake School, built in 1896 and listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Many nearby homes are farmhouses and rural lots, and older buildings often have older plumbing. Housing totals 1,717 units, and about 9 percent were vacant in 2020.

No city water: wells and private water companies

According to the Minnesota Department of Health, Scandia has no municipal water system. Residents rely on individual wells and private companies, mainly Scandia Water Company and Hilltop Water Company. The two primary systems draw from wells about 300 feet deep in the Jordan and Prairie du Chien-Jordan aquifers.

For a homeowner, that means water quality and pressure depend on a well or a small system, and the right equipment depends on a test. Read the water softener and filtration page before buying anything. A pressure tank or pump problem is a plumbing call, and leak detection helps when a bill or pump cycling points to a hidden leak.

Septic in Washington County

Scandia is in Washington County, whose Public Health and Environment office regulates septic permits, licensing, and inspections. The county requires a compliance inspection when a property with a septic system is sold, unless a compliance inspection was filed in the last three years or a new system has a certificate of compliance from the last five.

If you are buying or selling a Scandia home, ask about the septic inspection early. A slow drain or gurgling can point at a full tank or a failing drain field, and the plumber can start with drain cleaning to rule out a clogged line.

Getting to Scandia

Minnesota Highways 95 and 97 are the main routes, and the city sits halfway between Stillwater and Taylors Falls, near William O'Brien State Park. Marine on St. Croix is just south. Distances on rural roads vary, so we do not promise a specific arrival time, but same-day appointments are offered when available.
