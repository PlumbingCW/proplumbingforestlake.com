If water is dripping through a ceiling, soaking a wall, or the meter keeps spinning with every tap off, treat it as an emergency, and the 24/7 line is open now. Shut the main water valve if you can reach it, turn off power to any wet area, and move belongings out of the way.

When a hidden leak is an emergency

Quick test: turn off every fixture and appliance, then watch the water meter. If the leak indicator or the dial moves, water is escaping somewhere between the meter and the fixtures.

A high water bill with no change in use

City meters in Forest Lake are read quarterly, so a leak can run for weeks before a bill shows it.

Damp or stained walls and ceilings

Brown rings, bubbling paint, or a musty smell mean water has been moving behind the surface.

A warm spot on the floor

Warm patches on a slab can point at a leaking hot water line under the concrete.

The sound of running water

Hissing or running sounds with everything off point at a pressurized leak.

How a plumber finds the leak

Meter and isolation test

Closing valves in stages shows whether the leak is on the street side, inside the house, or on a single branch.

Acoustic listening

Sensitive microphones and ground sensors pick up the sound of water escaping under pressure.

Thermal imaging

An infrared camera shows temperature differences from moisture or a leaking hot water line.

Pressure test and camera

Isolated lines are pressurized to confirm a leak, and a camera can look inside drains.

None of these tools gives an answer on its own. The plumber combines them, because moisture behind a wall can travel and show up far from the source. A reading is confirmed before anything is opened.

Slab leaks, wall leaks, and leaks under the house

A slab leak happens in a supply line that runs under or through a concrete floor. It can show up as a warm floor, a high bill, or a sound of water. Fixing it may mean tunneling, breaking the slab, or rerouting the line overhead, and the right choice depends on the pipe and the layout.

Leaks in walls and ceilings usually involve a supply fitting, a failed shutoff, or a drain joint. A crawlspace leak can cause mold and wood rot before it is noticed. In each case the detection step decides how big the repair needs to be, and the repair itself may fall under burst pipe repair or repiping if the pipe is worn out.

Water meter and high-bill leaks

The City of Forest Lake reads its meters quarterly and wirelessly, and every home on city water has one. A running toilet flapper, a leaking irrigation line, or a break in the service line between the meter and the house can all add up quietly, so the meter test is where the search starts.

If the leak turns out to be on the supply pipe that runs from the street to the house, water service line repair covers what the fix involves. The city says its crews can shut the curb stop to tell which side of it a leak is on.

Safety steps before the plumber arrives

  • Close the main water valve if you see water actively running.
  • Stay away from wet areas near outlets, panels, and appliances.
  • Move belongings off wet floors and photograph stains with a date.
  • Do not cut into walls or floors before the source is found.

Who operates the street valve

According to the City of Forest Lake Water Department, the curb stop at the street is operated by city staff only. Do not count on it to stop water in an emergency, and close the main valve inside your home instead.
