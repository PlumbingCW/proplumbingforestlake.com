How to choose the right service

If water is leaking right now, start with burst pipe repair or leak detection. If a drain is backing up, start with drain cleaning and look at sewer line cleaning when the whole house is affected. For hot water, repair and replacement cover most cases.

For planned work, look at plumbing installation, repiping, and water softener and filtration. The local rules that shape the work, such as the sump discharge ordinance and the city sewer responsibility split, are explained on the pages where they apply.

Safety steps before the plumber arrives

  • Close the main water valve for any active leak.
  • Stop using drains and toilets during a sewer backup.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911.
  • Keep children and pets away from sewage and from wet electrical areas.

Checking a plumber's license

According to the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry, plumbing contractors must hold a state license, with a responsible master plumber and a surety bond. The license lookup at dli.mn.gov lets you confirm any plumber before you hire.
