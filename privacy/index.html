Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you call or visit this site.

Talk to a plumber
(651) 946-0134
Call Now

This policy explains what information Forest Lake Pro Plumbing collects when you visit this website or call our phone line, and how we use it. We keep it simple because this site does not use forms or accounts.

Information we collect

When you call, we may collect your phone number, your address, and a description of your plumbing problem so we can schedule service. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

When you visit the site, standard server logs record technical details such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you view. This site does not have a contact form or user accounts.

How we use information

  • To schedule and carry out plumbing service.
  • To improve the quality of our service and train staff.
  • To keep this website secure and working.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Sharing information

We share your details with the plumber assigned to your job so they can contact you and arrive prepared. We share information with service providers who help us run the phone line and website. We share information when the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic cookies or analytics to understand traffic. You can turn off cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by calling the number on this site or emailing the address in the footer. We will respond as the law requires.
